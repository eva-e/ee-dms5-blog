Stage 1 Collaboration Update - Week 5:

'a Living Masterpiece' by Calvin Araya:
This week Calvin and I discussed the sound design and score, specifically a variety of references and his animation progress. Currently, Calvin has completed the storyboarding, written draft and sketches for his animation. Below I have included a few notes from Calvin regarding the SFX, written draft and music pacing. This coming week, I will be watching the first draft of the animation and taking notes on my ideation for the scoring.

  • There are points where the music can either continue or drop down to give you a break. The animation is 3 minutes long.
  • Potential SFX: Bird flapping, death bird noises, doors shut/open, footsteps, nature, fireplace crackling, big vines growing, etc. These are all optional, it would be best to place these in after the base ost is applied to the animatic, so we can use sound to fill in spaces, rather than distract from the ost.
