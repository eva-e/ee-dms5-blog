Composer Research Task - Joe Hisaishi

Joe Hisaishi - Princess Mononoke

Joe Hisaishi is a minimalist and orchestral composer, known for composing the Studio Ghibli animation soundtracks, by Hayao Miyazaki. Hisaishi composed ‘The Legend of Ashitaka’ from ‘Princess Mononoke’, one of my most admired soundtracks. There are several other films that Hisaishi composed for, as well as solo works. Some of my favourite soundtracks include DA-MA-SHI-E, Water Traveller and Castle in the Sky. Prior to composing for film, he was inspired by Japanese electronic artist, Yellow Magic Orchestra. Hisaishi worked as a solo artist producing minimalist synthesiser and percussive pieces. Years later, his prior experience with synthesisers also carried into the soundtrack for 'Castle in the Sky', which he composed mostly on synthesisers, as well as orchestral arrangements to meet studio demands. In 2020, Joe Hisaishi mentioned how he takes a broad view approach when composing, as, "The first step is to look at the script, the scenario, and to work out what the world of that film is in the broadest sense.”
Prior to composing for Princess Mononoke, Hisaishi created an image album of musical sketches and his creative process based on the notes Hayao Miyazaki provided to him. The album establishes the emotional themes of the animation, including pre-recorded tracks which Joe Hisaishi composed before the final compositions.
