Wednesday, March 18

Rhythm Task

This week we delved into rhythm and its impact on the emotional journey through sound. Rhythm in soundtrack and music is able to change our perception of the modd of a scene through its tempo, velocity and pattern. Our task for Week 4 was to compose a track which had elements of rhythm, using the Kontakt Patterns Tool. The first task was to use any instrument and create a starting pattern. From this pattern, it evolved into a track of multiple instrument layers, with varied patterns. I used the drawing tool to add bars or remove a few per instrument. The first layer instrument was a bass synth rhythm. I used the same pattern from the bass synth and changed one of the bars for the Jacob Collier Choir. In some instances towards the end, I was playing the notes manually on the keyboard instead of using the pattern, such as the clap sound and drum kicks, creating a dynamic and extra melodic layers. Most instruments used were following a rhythmic pattern I had drawn on the Patterns tool.
