Week 5 Reflection:

Wednesday, April 1

Paulstretch Experiment Task

This week, I began experimenting using Paulstretch, first with my rhythm track, manipulating the stretch amount and frequency shift. I increased the stretch amount to 2.4 and frequency shift to 48, slowing the track down dramatically, consequently changing the length to 3 minutes from a minute thirty.

I then loaded my harmony track, making the same adjustments to. My harmony track sounded a little distorted and uncanny. A little discomforting to listen to after stretching the original track. The chords lacked clarity and consistency, as they became delayed and moving as waves. The full chord progression wasn't clear. The third track I utilised was my timbre track. While this track wasn't perfected for the timbre task, I decided to use it in Paulstretch. There was a common sound between the three tracks after their stretched adjustments, a bell tolling like sound. I imagined myself at the bottom of a bell tower, the vibration of the bell ringing and bouncing acorss surfaces.

I loaded an additional track for variation, as I wanted to experiment with a musical piece I composed this year called 'With Love'. The track incudes guitar, bass and piano instruments. In Paulstretch I changed the stretch amount to 0.1, with speeding the song in mind. Originally the song has a slow tempo, intended to be calming and hopeful. However after altering the time and pacing, the track became a little overwhelming, similar to a beautiful moment vanishing or rapidly changing. For the fifth time stretch export, I stretched my final track again, increasing the stretch amount to 2.9. The track now resembled a weeping willow, swaying in the wind. I envisioned closing my eyes and listening to the breeze. It was the drastic change in pacing and extreme stretching of the track that altered how the emotion is expressed. The snare was extremely stretched to the point where it sounded like the breeze on a windy day.

The final composition included, layering and editing parts of the exported tracks, to form a 2 minute composition. I also recieved feedback this week from Jack, regarding the genre of sound that the paulstretch track created:"I really liked how you used paulstretch to create an ethereal pad sound, it created a really magical and relaxing atmosphere. I also liked the way you created rhythmic sounds, and how you used shorter time stretching to create interesting rhythms."
