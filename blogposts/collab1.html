Stage 1 Collaboration Update - Week 5:

'The Bird and The Branches' by Pita Khanngern:
This week Pita and I discussed the sound effects and score for the Denial Stage. Pita sent me the first update of her game, a short playthrough/vertical slice of the Denial stage. The game currently has placeholder sound effects which are from free sound libraries, used for my reference. I recieved a list of sound effects and was also sent a Game Details Document from Pita, which outlines each level/stage of the game. Download Pita's Game Details Document

  • Sound Effects: Branch Bounce/Echo, Struggle Friction in Anger Stage, Misfire/Error Glitch Bird flapping sound, The Bird's Footsteps, The Beak Peck.
