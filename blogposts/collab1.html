'The Bird and The Branches' by Pita Khanngern:

    • Stage 2 Collaboration Update - Week 6:

    'The Bird and The Branches' by Pita Khanngern:
    During week 6 I focused on gathering sound effects through foley. Rather than sourcing from sound libraries I decided to experiment and attempt to make foley sounds. I was once tasked with creating foley sounds for Sound Design for Digital Media in first year, which required immense attention to detail. I utilised Pita's first play through video to assist in my sound designing. Below I have attached a few different sounds I made with objects found at home, they are draft foley sounds. Last week Pita gave me a list of sounds that she will need in her game. I went by this list to create sounds.

  • Branch Bounce/Echo
  • Struggle Friction in Anger Stage
  • Misfire/Error Glitch
  • Bird flapping sound
  • The Bird's Footsteps
  • The Beak Peck

    • Branch Bounce/Leaves - Dried leaves and flowers, shuffling and pushing the leaves





    Bird Flapping - Moving a traditional Chinese wooden fan up and down on a soft flannelette doona surface





    Tree Leaves Sound - Shuffliing dry flowers





    Bird Footsteps - Tapping the edge of the wooden fan





    Bird Woodpeck - Tapping the top wooden surface of my piano with the edge of my finger nail
