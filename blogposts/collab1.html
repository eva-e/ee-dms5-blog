    Stage 4 Collaboration Update - Week 7:

    'The Bird and The Branches' by Pita Khanngern:
    This week, after reciveing feedback during my progress presentation, I decided to start the soundtrack of the Denial Stage. While Pita mentioned she wasn't in a rush, I had to also take into consideration my deadline and amount of time that is remaining to recieve feedback. I began by listening to each reference song and taking notes while watching the playthrough Pita sent. The atmosphere of this level looks quite sinister and drawn, a little foggy. Pita described this level as muffled and repressed. A few tracks from C418 came to mind, including Thirteen and Excuse. They both carry a repressive and sorrowful tone. Pita mentioned Subwoofer melody suits the Denial stage, however she was happy for suggestions also. As I have not discussed the soundtrack further since we met in person, I started by working on the draft track with the three references in mind. This week I focused on ideating the soundtrack, to then move forward next week and compose.

    Stage 9 Collaboration Update - Week 12:

    'The Bird and The Branches' by Pita Khanngern:
    In Week 12, I sent Pita the draft and continued working on the first draft, as she liked the melody. Pita suggested variation in instruments while keeping the same melody for the Denial stage. The simplicity of the notes for Denial can then gradually be emphasised and intensified in the Anger stage. Rather than multiple melodies, Pita preferred different instruments for the same melody in Denial. I proceeded to compose with a new instrument, following the same melody. I added a synth/ethereal instrument called Beautiful Unease, from Absynth 6, in order to reflect the new visuals Pita gave me this week, which are vibrant and include floating particles.
